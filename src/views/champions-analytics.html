What does “consistency” mean?

Consistency = 100 ÷ (1 + coefficient of variation) × reporting rate. The coefficient of variation is daily step standard deviation divided by daily mean. Individuals use their reported daily steps. Teams use daily steps per reporter, weighted by that day’s reporter count. A perfectly steady, perfectly reported challenge scores 100.
