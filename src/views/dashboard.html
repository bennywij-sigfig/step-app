About Trotter

Gemini processes your message, recent local conversation context, and screenshots you choose to upload. For our usage they delete input after 72h and do not use it for training. This web app does not store screenshots or server-side chat history.

Trotter can misread an image or phrase a fact incorrectly. Please review extracted dates and step counts before saving.

Use this only on a private device. Trotter still sends only recent context—not the full saved transcript—to Gemini.
